# SWYNEX - Task 4: Final Data Analytics Project
## Employee Salary & Workforce Analysis

### 1. Problem Statement
The company wanted to understand whether its employee salary structure is fair or not, which department has the highest cost, and from which city most hiring is done.

### 2. Dataset Information
- Total Employees: 21
- Columns: Name, Age, City, Department, Salary, Joining_Date
- Departments: Sales, IT, Marketing, HR, Finance
- Cities: 12 Cities
- Source: Employee Dataset provided in Task 3

### 3. Data Cleaning Process
- Removed blank rows
- Checked and removed duplicate / missing values
- Converted Joining_Date into proper date format
- Converted Salary and Age into number format

### 4. Dashboard Created
Created an interactive dashboard in MS Excel showing:
- Total Employees: 21
- Total Salary: 11,19,000
- Average Salary: 53,286
- Average Age: 30
- City-wise Employee Distribution (Pie Chart)
- Department-wise Average Salary (Bar Chart)

### 5. Key Business Insights
- IT department has the highest average salary: Rs. 64,400
- Finance department has the second highest average salary: Rs. 64,000
- Sales department has the maximum employees (6) but the lowest average salary: Rs. 46,000
- Delhi is the main hiring hub with 6 employees, followed by Mumbai with 3 employees
- The average age of employees is 30 years, which shows the team is young and fresh
- Maximum hiring was done in the year 2023
- Highest salary is Rs. 75,000 (IT, Delhi) and lowest salary is Rs. 41,000 (Marketing, Jaipur)

### 6. Recommendations
- Review the salary structure of the Sales team as they have the lowest average salary
- Focus hiring in Delhi and Mumbai as most talent is available there
- Maintain the balance between experienced and fresh employees
